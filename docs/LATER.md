# RPS1 — Later

Good ideas that are not in the MVP.

Parking something here is a decision, not a loss. It is how the MVP
stays small enough to actually ship by Session 3.

- Staff accounts (operators who log loads themselves)
- Farmer and admin roles
- Warehouse storage + rice expiry (1-2 month shelf life)
- Reports (income, volume processed, occupancy, expiry)
- Offline support (work without internet, sync on reconnect)
- Payment methods: cash / online / card / e-wallet, with references/receipts
- Automatic package pricing (per-sack rate) instead of a hand-typed price
- Harvesting as a tracked stage
- Receipts and payment-gateway integration
- Recording moisture per load
- Editing a load's details after saving
- Skipping stages automatically for drying-only / milling-only loads
