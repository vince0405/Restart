# RPS1 — Later

Good ideas that are not in the MVP.

Parking something here is a decision, not a loss. It is how the MVP
stays small enough to actually ship by Session 3.

## Next up (the first thing to build after the MVP)
- **Operator login**: operators move loads through the steps themselves,
  instead of telling the Monitor

## Later
- **Customer login**: farmers follow their own loads (must only ever see
  their own)
- Admin role
- Storage locations and capacity (where in the warehouse, how full)
- Storing between steps (e.g. dried palay waiting to be milled)
- A separate storage fee (for now it is part of the agreed price)
- Reports (income, volume processed, occupancy, expiry)
- Offline support (work without internet, sync on reconnect)
- Payment methods: cash / online / card / e-wallet, with references/receipts
- Automatic package pricing (per-sack rate) instead of a hand-typed price
- Receipts and payment-gateway integration
- Recording moisture per load
- Editing a load's details after saving

## From the Figma design (shown in the mock-up, not built)
- Farmer / Operator / Admin role switcher and per-role dashboards
- Warehouse bins with capacity bars (e.g. "Bin R-02 95% full")
- Billing page: invoices, payment history, payment methods (cash, online,
  card, GCash/Maya), reference numbers and printable receipts
- Itemised prices per service step and per-kg rates
- Reports page (income, palay processed, rice yield, charts)
- Roles & permissions page, invite user
- Customer profiles (phone number, barangay) and customer search
- "Working offline" banner and sync status
- Notifications bell
- Extra intake fields: moisture %, palay variety, vehicle plate, notes
- Drying and milling records (temperatures, target moisture, rice yield)
- Multi-step intake wizard (the MVP uses one form with the same sections)
